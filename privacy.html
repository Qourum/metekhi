Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

"Metekhi — Host of Kings" is a castle-defence game that runs in the browser (qourum.github.io/metekhi) and as an Android app on Google Play. This page says what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

  • The game asks for no account, name, email or any other personal data.
  • Your progress (battles, coins, diamonds, items, settings) is stored only on your device, in the browser's local storage. We cannot see it.
  • The game uses no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking cookies. There are no ads in the game.

Purchases on Google Play

Purchases in the Android app, where available, go through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment details (card, address); we never receive them. To confirm a purchase, our server receives the purchase token, the product code, the order number and a random code for the app installation that says nothing about who you are. We keep these records so that one purchase is never counted twice and refunds are recorded. We share them with no one. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The web version

The web version is served by GitHub Pages and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Both services may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub and Google policies. So that the game also works offline, its pictures are kept on your device.

Playing together online

In the “Play together” mode the game between two players goes through Supabase’s servers (European Union, Frankfurt) and, where possible, directly between the two devices (WebRTC). Only what the game needs is sent: the nickname you choose, the room code, your king, your heroes’ levels and the moves of the battle. None of it is stored, and it is gone when the room closes. Over a direct connection the two devices see each other’s IP address — that is how WebRTC works; Google’s and Cloudflare’s STUN servers may be used to set up the connection. We only count how many rooms are opened each day, without any identity. See Supabase’s policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

Deleting your data

Your progress lives on your device: clearing the app's or the site's data deletes it for good. If you want us to delete the record of a purchase too, write to us with its order number (GPA.…).

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date on this page changes. Questions and requests: github.com/Qourum/metekhi/issues.
